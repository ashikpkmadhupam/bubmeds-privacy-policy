BubMeds Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

BubMeds helps caregivers organize medicine reminders and record whether doses were taken or skipped. This policy explains what the app handles and how it is stored.

Designed for on-device use

Information stored in BubMeds

Information you enter is stored on your device: caregiver name and phone number; patient names, ages and optional photos; medicine names, descriptions, routes, doses and reminder schedules; reminder preferences; and dose actions such as taken or skipped.

How information is used

BubMeds uses this information only to display the profiles and medicine plan you create, schedule local alarms and notifications, and show dose history and adherence summaries.

Storage, backup and sharing

Profiles, medicine details, photos and dose history are kept in BubMeds’ private app storage. BubMeds does not provide an account, operate a sync server, or send this information to BubMeds or analytics or advertising services. Android device backup or device-transfer features may copy app data according to your Android settings; BubMeds does not receive that backup.

Photos and permissions

You may choose a photo from your gallery or take a photo for a patient or medicine profile. The selected image is copied into BubMeds’ private app storage. BubMeds does not access photos unless you choose one. Notifications and exact-alarm access are used to deliver reminders at the times you configure. Full-screen notification access is used to show the alarm screen when a reminder rings.

Retention and deletion

Your entries remain on the device until you remove them, clear BubMeds’ app storage, or uninstall the app. Some past dose records may remain in the report after a patient or medicine is removed. To erase all BubMeds data, use Android Settings → Apps → BubMeds → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Android-managed backups may have separate retention controls in your Google account settings.

Children and patient information

BubMeds is intended for caregivers, not for children to use independently. A caregiver may enter a child’s name, age or photo to organize care. Please enter only information you are authorized to manage.

Health information

BubMeds is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend treatment, or replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist. Follow the prescribed medicine instructions and consult a healthcare professional about schedule changes.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as BubMeds changes. The current version will be available in the app and on its Google Play listing. For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email shown on the BubMeds Google Play Store page.
